ACME CORP EMPLOYEE HEALTH PLAN — SUMMARY OF BENEFITS (PLAN YEAR 2026)

PLAN TYPE
This is a Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) plan. You may see any provider,
but you pay less when you use in-network providers.

DEDUCTIBLE
Individual in-network deductible: $2,000 per plan year.
Family in-network deductible: $4,000 per plan year.
The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services before the plan begins
to pay. Preventive care is not subject to the deductible.

COINSURANCE
After you meet your deductible, you pay 20% coinsurance for most in-network
covered services and the plan pays 80%. Out-of-network coinsurance is 40%.

OUT-OF-POCKET MAXIMUM
Individual in-network out-of-pocket maximum: $6,000 per plan year.
Once you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100% of covered
in-network services for the rest of the plan year.

PREVENTIVE CARE
In-network preventive care is covered at 100% with no deductible and no copay.
This includes annual wellness visits, routine immunizations, routine screenings,
and preventive lab work such as routine venipuncture (blood draw, code 36415)
performed as part of a covered screening.

OFFICE VISITS
Primary care office visit (code 99213): $25 copay after deductible.
Specialist office visit: $50 copay after deductible.

LABORATORY AND DIAGNOSTICS
In-network diagnostic lab work such as a comprehensive metabolic panel
(code 80053) is covered at 80% after the deductible is met.

SURGERY AND HOSPITALIZATION
Inpatient hospital stays and outpatient surgery are covered at 80% after the
deductible. Prior authorization is required for scheduled inpatient procedures
and most outpatient surgeries, including knee arthroscopy and joint procedures.
Failure to obtain prior authorization may result in a $500 penalty.

EMERGENCY SERVICES
Emergency room visits are covered at 80% after a $300 ER facility copay. The
copay is waived if you are admitted to the hospital.

PRESCRIPTION DRUGS
Tier 1 (generic): $10 copay. Tier 2 (preferred brand): $40 copay.
Tier 3 (non-preferred brand): $75 copay. Specialty drugs: 25% coinsurance up to
$250 per fill.

DUPLICATE AND BILLING ERRORS
If you are billed twice for the same service on the same date, or billed for a
preventive service that should be covered at 100%, you may dispute the charge.
Contact HR benefits support to open a billing review.
